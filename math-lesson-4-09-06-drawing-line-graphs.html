Readiness Check: Count Up the Scale
The bottom line is 0. Count up from there.
~3 min

The bottom line of a graph is 0. Type the value of each line.

Counting by 5, the 7th line above 0 is
✅ 6 and 35.
Counting by 2: 2, 4, 6. Counting by 5: 5, 10, 15, 20, 25, 30, 35. The step you count by is the scale. Today you get to choose it.
🔄 Skip-count from 0.
The 1st line above 0 is one step. The 2nd line is two steps. Multiply the line number by the step: 3 × 2 for the first one, 7 × 5 for the second.

📐 What You Just Saw

1. Scale. Start at 0. Count by a step that reaches the biggest value and lets each point land on a line.

2. Plot. Find the label at the bottom, then go straight up to the value.

3. Join. Connect the points with straight lines, left to right.

4. Name it. Add a title and a label on each side.

Worked Example: Reading the Graph You Drew
Back to Pip from the Warm-Up
Problem

Here is Pip’s finished graph. In which month did Pip gain the most weight? How many pounds?

Step 1. Find the steepest piece of the line. It’s month 3 to month 4.
Step 2. Read both ends: 14 pounds and 22 pounds.
Step 3. Subtract. 22 − 14 = 8 pounds.
Check. The other pieces: 8 − 4 = 4, 14 − 8 = 6, 26 − 22 = 4. None is bigger than 8. ✓

✅ That scale works.
Counting by 3 (0 to 18) puts every value right on a line. Counting by 2 (0 to 18 or 20) also works: 9 lands halfway between 8 and 10, which is easy to see. Both reach 18 without going way past it.
