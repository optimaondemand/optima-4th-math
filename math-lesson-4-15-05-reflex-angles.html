📐 What You Just Saw

A full turn is 360°. An angle that turns more than 180° but less than 360° is a reflex angle.

Two rays always make two angles, and together they make a full turn. So the outside (reflex) angle is 360° − the inside angle: 360° − 100° = 260°.

The Whole Angle Family
From no turn to a full turn
NameMeasureAs a turn
Acutemore than 0°, less than 90°less than a quarter turn
Rightexactly 90°a quarter turn
Obtusemore than 90°, less than 180°between a quarter and a half turn
Straightexactly 180°a half turn (“a 180”)
Reflexmore than 180°, less than 360°more than a half turn, less than a full turn
Full turnexactly 360°all the way around (“a 360”)
Watch out: For the outside angle, subtract from 360, not 180. 180 − 100 = 80 gives the angle next to it on a straight line. 360 − 100 = 260 gives the reflex angle that goes all the way around.

Worked Example: The Clock at 2:00
The long way around the clock
Problem

At 2:00, the small angle between the clock hands is 60°. What is the reflex angle between the hands?

Picture both angles. The small angle goes from 12 to 2. The reflex angle goes the long way, from 2 all the way around to 12.
Use the full turn. Small + reflex = 360°.
Subtract. 360 − 60 = 300.
Answer. The reflex angle is 300°.
Check. 60 + 300 = 360 ✓, and 300 is between 180 and 360, so it’s reflex ✓
