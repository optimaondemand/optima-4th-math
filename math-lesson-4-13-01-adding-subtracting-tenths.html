✅ 0.6 and 2.5.
No whole ones and 6 tenths is 0.6. Two whole ones and 5 tenths is 2.5. The ones go before the decimal point. The tenths go right after it.
🔄 Ones, point, tenths.
The decimal point is the dot that sits between the whole ones and the tenths. Write the number of ones first (write 0 if there are none), then the point, then the number of tenths.

📐 What You Just Saw

You add and subtract tenths the same way you add and subtract anything: count them. 3 tenths + 4 tenths = 7 tenths, so 0.3 + 0.4 = 0.7.

Ones go with ones. Tenths go with tenths. In 1.2 + 1.5, add the ones (1 + 1 = 2) and the tenths (2 + 5 = 7). The answer is 2.7.

When you write the problem in columns, line up the decimal points. Then the ones sit over the ones and the tenths sit over the tenths.

Step 1. Tenths: 7 + 2 = 9. Ones: 0 + 1 = 1. The pitcher has 1.9 L.
Step 2. Tenths: 9 − 5 = 4. Ones: 1 − 1 = 0. There is 0.4 L left.
Check. Does it make sense? The guests drank most of the punch, so a small amount, less than half a liter, is left. ✓
