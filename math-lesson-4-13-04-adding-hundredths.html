✅ 4 tenths and 7 hundredths. 10 hundredths = 1 tenth.
The first digit after the decimal point is tenths. The second is hundredths. And 10 hundredths make 1 tenth, the same way 10 pennies make 1 dime.
🔄 Two places after the point.
In 0.47, the 4 is in the tenths place (first after the point) and the 7 is in the hundredths place (second after the point). For the last one, think: how many pennies make a dime?

📐 What You Just Saw

Line up the decimal points. Then add one place at a time, starting with the smallest: hundredths, then tenths, then ones.

If a place adds up to 10 or more, trade 10 for 1 in the next place to the left. 10 hundredths → 1 tenth. 10 tenths → 1 one.

0.48 + 0.36: hundredths 8 + 6 = 14 → write 4, trade 1 tenth. Tenths 4 + 3 + 1 = 8. Answer: 0.84.

Watch the zero: 7 pennies is 0.07, not 0.7. 0.7 would be 7 dimes, which is 70 cents. The 0 holds the tenths place open so the 7 lands in the hundredths place.

Estimate. About 2 and a half + about 2 = about 4 and a half, or a little less.
Hundredths. 8 + 5 = 13. Write 3, trade 10 hundredths for 1 tenth.
Tenths. 4 + 7 + 1 = 12. Write 2, trade 10 tenths for 1 one.
Ones. 2 + 1 + 1 = 4. Both jars hold 4.23 dollars.
