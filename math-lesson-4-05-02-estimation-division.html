Today's Mission
Yesterday's number bonds work when the dividend splits cleanly. Today: estimate ANY dividend by rounding three different ways. Compare the three estimates to the actual quotient — and learn which rounding gives the closest answer.
❓
Essential Question
For 743 ÷ 3, which rounding gives the closest estimate — 600, 750, or 900? Why?

743 isn't a nice number to divide. So you ROUND it first. But to what?

600 ÷ 3 = 200. 750 ÷ 3 = 250. 900 ÷ 3 = 300. The actual quotient is 247 r 2. So 750 ÷ 3 = 250 is the closest — because 750 is closest to 743 AND 3 divides 750 evenly.

A community boating center had $200. It bought 6 identical life vests for its customers and had $8 left. Estimate the cost of one life vest. (Hint: $200 − $8 = $192 spent. Then $192 ÷ 6 = cost per vest.)

$200 − $8 = $192. Now divide: $192 ÷ 6. Round 192 to 180 (multiple of 6): 180 ÷ 6 = $30. So each vest costs about $30 — close to $32 exact.

Compute each estimated quotient. Compare them to the actual 247 r 2.
Estimates: 600 ÷ 3 = 200 (low). 750 ÷ 3 = 250 (closest to actual 247). 900 ÷ 3 = 300 (high). The divisor-friendly rounding (750) gave the tightest estimate. Rounding too far down or up lost precision.
📐 Three Estimates, One Closest

For any division:

1. Round DOWN to a low friendly value → LOW estimate.

2. Round to a DIVISOR-FRIENDLY value (a multiple of the divisor) → usually CLOSEST estimate.

3. Round UP to a high friendly value → HIGH estimate.

The actual quotient sits somewhere in the middle. The divisor-friendly estimate hugs it most tightly because it keeps the most of the original dividend AND divides cleanly.

Worked Example: 878 ÷ 9
Estimate, then long division.
Problem

Find 878 ÷ 9. Make TWO estimates first (round 878 differently each time, including one with both numbers rounded). Then compute the exact quotient with long division.

Watch the Math Crew solve it together:

⚖️ Step by Step: 878 ÷ 9
Estimate: 900 ÷ 9 = 100 (divisor-friendly).
Step 1. 87 ÷ 9 = 9 r 6. Write 9.
Step 2. Bring down 8. 68 ÷ 9 = 7 r 5. Write 7.
Final: 97 r 5. Check: 97 × 9 + 5 = 873 + 5 = 878. ✓

Three estimates frame the actual. Long division finds the truth. Now apply.
