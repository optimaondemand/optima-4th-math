📐 What You Just Saw

The same rule keeps working. Each new place is ten times the one to its right.

10 thousands make 1 ten-thousand. 10 ten-thousands make 1 hundred-thousand. And — once we get there — 10 hundred-thousands make 1 million.

The comma in 472,830 sits between thousands and hundreds. Everything left of the comma is the thousands period. Everything right of the comma is the ones period. Read the left side as a number, then say "thousand," then read the right side. That is how you read any six-digit number.

⚠️

About 1,000,000: It has two commas, not one. The first comma splits millions from thousands. The second comma splits thousands from hundreds. Both commas help us read the number — they group the digits into threes.
