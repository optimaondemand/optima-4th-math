Math Manipulatives Library

Hand students a manipulative, any time, for any problem. These are open, on-screen versions of every tool in the Dimensions Math kits from Pre-K to Grade 5, plus the bar models, number bonds and number lines from the books. Share a link, put one on a Canvas page, or project it.

    • Tool demonstrations

How to use it with students Every link you share is a student link: just the tool, no way back here. Open this for the three ways to use it.

1Hand out a blank tool

Give students the manipulative itself, ready for any problem: the lesson, the workbook, homework or just exploring.

  1. Open any tool below.
  2. Tap Share right away, without changing anything.
  3. Send the student link, show the QR code, or paste the embed code into a Canvas page.

Want just the tool with no directions? Untick Include the how-to directions in the Share box.

See a blank disc mat ↗

2Send a problem you set up ahead of time

Build the starting point yourself, then share it. Students open the link and see exactly what you made. Start over brings back your setup, not a blank tool.

  1. Open a tool and set up the problem.
  2. Tap Share.
  3. Send that link. Every student gets your setup.
Examples you can try: 2,407 already built on the disc mat, ready for students to take away 1,568. Or a 4 × 6 array of red counters for students to split into equal groups.

3Get students’ work back

On a student link, the top button is Turn in my work. It makes a picture of the student’s tool and a link that opens their work exactly as they left it.

  1. Students build their answer, then tap Turn in my work.
  2. They copy or download the picture, or copy the link.
  3. They paste it where you ask: a Canvas assignment (File Upload or Website URL), a discussion, or the chat.

Open a student’s link to see their model live, then move the pieces together while you give feedback.

4More ways to teach with it

  • Save setup keeps a tool you set up in My setups, ready to reuse or share again.
  • Problem sets: put 2–8 saved setups in order and send one link. Students tap Next.
  • Side by side: two tools on one screen, like discs next to place-value cards.
  • Flash it: show a tool for a few seconds, then hide it, for number talks. Suggested questions for you sit beside it. Students never see them.
  • Surprise me gives students a random problem to build.
  • Pen draws on top of any tool. Undo and Redo fix mistakes.
  • Read to me reads the student directions aloud, and the 🔊 beside the directions line reads that line and the total.
  • Say it first: tick it in the Share box and the total stays covered until the student taps I said it.
  • Directions in Spanish: tick it in the Share box for the how-to steps and the Canvas directions in Spanish.
  • Games are two-player and whole-class games that run on these tools, with a rules card students can read.
