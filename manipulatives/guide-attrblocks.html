Attribute Blocks

A set of 60 blocks that differ in four ways. Students sort them and explain their rule.

2-minute readGrades K–1Kit: Attribute Blocks

What it is

Shape: circle, square, triangle, rectangle, hexagonColor: red, yellow, blueSize: big, smallThickness: thick, thin

Every block is one of each. No two blocks are the same.

Sorting builds the words students need to describe shapes, and the habit of saying why things belong together.

How to use the tool

  1. Pick Free, 2 hoops or Venn.
  2. Drag a block into a hoop.
  3. Tap a hoop's name to change the rule.
  4. Drag a block back to put it away.

Try this with your class

Guess my rule

  1. Secretly pick a rule, like "blue." Put 3 blocks in the hoop without saying it.
  2. Students hold up a block and ask, "Does this one belong?"
  3. When they think they know, they say the rule and prove it with one more block.

Next: the train game. Each new block must match the one before in exactly two ways. Students say which two.

Watch for

Too much at once.Start with only color and shape. Add size and thickness later.
Thick vs. thin.Hard to see. Let students feel the real blocks, or stack them side by side.
"Not red."Students may put only one color in that hoop. Ask: is yellow red? Then where does it go?
