Tangrams

Seven flat pieces cut from one square. Slide, turn and flip them to fill a shape.

2-minute readGrades K–4Kit: Tangrams

What it is

Tangrams build spatial sense: seeing how shapes fit, turn and combine. They also show area without numbers. Two small triangles cover the same space as the square, the medium triangle, or the parallelogram.

How to use the tool

  1. Drag a piece into the gray shape.
  2. Drag its round handle to turn it.
  3. Use the Flip button for the parallelogram. It's the only piece that needs flipping.
  4. Done when every piece fits with no gaps and no overlaps.

Try this with your class

Use the 2 small triangles to make the square. Then the medium triangle. Then the parallelogram.

  1. Students find all three can be built from the 2 small triangles.
  2. Ask: so which takes up more space, the square or the medium triangle? (Neither. They're the same.)
  3. Then try a full puzzle in the tool.

When a student is stuck

Start with the big pieces.The 2 large triangles have the fewest places they can go.
Look for corners.Ask: which piece could fit that pointy corner?
Hold off on the answer.Give one piece's spot, not the whole solution.
