Pattern Blocks

Six colored shapes with sides the same length, so they fit together. Great for designs, symmetry and fractions.

2-minute readGrades K–5Kit: Pattern Blocks

What it is

Yellow hexagonRed trapezoidBlue rhombusGreen triangleOrange squareTan rhombus

The first four fit inside the hexagon. That makes them a fraction kit.

Change the whole and every value changes. If the trapezoid is 1, the hexagon is 2 and the triangle is 1/3.

How to use the tool

  1. Drag blocks from the top onto the mat.
  2. Drag a block's round handle to turn it.
  3. Drag a block back to the top to put it away.

Try this with your class

Cover a hexagon as many different ways as you can.

  1. 2 trapezoids, 3 blue rhombuses, 6 triangles, and mixes of these.
  2. Write each as a sum: 1/2 + 1/2 = 1, 1/3 + 1/3 + 1/6 + 1/6 = 1.
  3. Ask: which one used the most blocks? Why is each block worth less?

Younger students: build a design with a line of symmetry. Fold-check it in your head: does each side match?

Watch for

Forgetting the whole."A triangle is 1/6" is only true when the hexagon is 1. Always name the whole.
The square and tan rhombus.They don't fit the hexagon evenly. Leave them out of fraction work.
More blocks means more.6 triangles and 1 hexagon are the same amount.
