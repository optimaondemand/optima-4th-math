Ten Frames & Part-Part-Whole

Two rows of five boxes. Students fill them with counters to see numbers to 10 and 20.

What it is

Two rows of five boxes. Fill the top row first, left to right.

Students learn to see amounts without counting one by one. That leads straight to making ten, the most useful mental-math move in the early grades.

The part-part-whole mat in the same tool splits a whole into two parts, like a number bond you can move.

How to use the tool

  1. Drag a counter into a box.
  2. Tap a counter to flip it red or yellow.
  3. Drag a counter out to take it away.
  4. Ask: how many? How many more make 10?

Try this with your class

Quick look

  1. Show a frame with 8 counters for 2 seconds, then hide it.
  2. Ask: how many? How did you see it?
  3. Listen for "5 and 3" or "2 less than 10". That's the goal.

Next: 8 + 5 on a double ten frame. Move 2 counters to fill the first frame. Now it's 10 + 3.

Watch for

Filling boxes in any order.Top row first, left to right. Then 5 and 10 are easy to see.
Counting one by one every time.Flash the frame quickly so there isn't time to count.
Two colors, one total.Red 6 and yellow 4 is still 10. Ask: how many in all?
