Warm-Up: Read the Scale
Job 1 · Check Mrs. Mabel's scale
Your job

Mrs. Mabel weighs everything she sells on her big store scale. It shows weight in two units: the pound (lb) is the big unit, and the ounce (oz) is the small one. The Smudge knocked the scale, so first, check it. How many ounces make 1 pound?

What to do
  1. Round 1: drag or tap 1-ounce weights onto the scale until the needle points to 1 lb. Watch the small marks light up and count them.
  2. Type how many ounces make 1 pound.
  3. Round 2: weigh the beans. Put on the 1-pound weight, then 1-ounce weights, and watch the needle move past 1 lb.
  4. Round 3: read the oats. Find the last pound number the needle passed, then count the small marks after it. Type the weight in pounds and ounces.
Look at the dial. The little marks between 0 and 1 lb are ounces. Count them!

The Math Crew: Two Ways
Which is heavier: the pineapple at 2 lb 9 oz, or the melon at 43 oz?

The Crew found the answer two different ways. Read both. Which way would you pick?

A
Atlas: pounds and ounces
"I change the melon's 43 oz to pounds and ounces. I count by 16s: 16, 32. The next one, 48, is too many. So I split 43 into 32 and 11. 32 oz is 2 lb, so the melon is 2 lb 11 oz. Both are 2 pounds, and 11 ounces is 2 more than 9 ounces. The melon is heavier by 2 oz."
F
Felix: all ounces
"I change the pineapple to ounces. 2 lb is 2 × 16 = 32 oz. 32 oz + 9 oz = 41 oz. The melon is 43 oz, and 43 − 41 = 2. The melon is heavier by 2 oz. Same answer!"
H
Hazel: picture the dial
"I picture Mrs. Mabel's scale. Every pound has 16 little marks, one for each ounce. So 2 pounds is 2 groups of 16 marks."
C
Cora: trade 16, not 10
"With feet we traded 12 inches. Here we trade 16 ounces for 1 pound. When the ounces reach 16, trade them!"
💡

Crew rule: Picture the dial (Hazel). Pick a way: pounds and ounces, or all ounces (Atlas, Felix). Trade 16 ounces for a pound and check the units (Cora).

Repack the Raisins: 45 oz = ? lb ? oz
Job 2 · Count by 16s and use a number bond
Your job

The Smudge dumped Mrs. Mabel's raisins into one big bin: 45 oz of them. She sells raisins in 1-pound bags, and her labels use pounds and ounces. How many pounds and ounces is 45 oz?

What to do
  1. Fill 1-pound bags: tap + Fill a 1 lb bag. Each bag holds 16 oz. Stop before you go past 45.
  2. Fill in the number bond: 45 oz splits into the ounces in full bags and the leftover ounces.
  3. Type the weight in pounds and ounces.
Each bag is 16 ounces. Count by 16s: 16, 32 … and stop before you go past 45.

Your job

A customer wants nuts and dried apples in one basket. The nuts weigh 1 lb 12 oz. The apples weigh 2 lb 9 oz. How much does the whole order weigh?

What to do

Study It: How Mrs. Mabel Split Her Sugar
Already solved for you. Read it, then split a sack yourself.

👀 Nothing to type here. This problem is solved two ways. Both ways get the same answer. Read the one that makes more sense to you.

Problem

Mrs. Mabel has a sack of sugar that weighs 8 lb 3 oz. She scoops out 1 lb 10 oz. How much sugar is left in the sack?

You can't take 10 ounces away from 3 ounces. So first, trade 1 pound for 16 ounces.
Way 1: pounds and ounces
  1. Trade 1 pound for 16 ounces. 8 lb 3 oz becomes 7 lb 19 oz.
  2. Take away the ounces: 19 oz − 10 oz = 9 oz.
  3. Take away the pounds: 7 lb − 1 lb = 6 lb.
  4. The sugar left is 6 lb 9 oz.
Way 2: all ounces
  1. 8 lb 3 oz is 131 oz. (8 × 16 = 128, and 3 more)
  2. 1 lb 10 oz is 26 oz. (1 × 16 = 16, and 10 more)
  3. Take away: 131 oz − 26 oz = 105 oz.
  4. Change back: 96 oz make 6 lb, and 9 oz are left. The sugar left is 6 lb 9 oz.

Check it: put the scoop back. 6 lb 9 oz + 1 lb 10 oz = 7 lb 19 oz. 19 oz is 1 lb 3 oz, so that is 8 lb 3 oz, the whole sack. ✓

Split the Rice: 5 lb 3 oz − 2 lb 7 oz
Job 5 · Take away on the Trade Mat
Your job

A sack of rice weighs 5 lb 3 oz. Mrs. Mabel scoops out 2 lb 7 oz for the school kitchen. How much rice is left in the sack?

What to do
  1. Build the sack on the mat: 5 pound discs and 3 ounce discs.
  2. Take away the pounds first: move 2 pound discs to the take-away box.
  3. Take away 7 ounces. There are only 3, so break 1 pound into 16 ounces first, then move 7.
  4. Read what is left on the mat. Type it in pounds and ounces.
Not enough ounces to take away? Break 1 pound into 16 ounces. Then there are plenty.

Last Job: Is 3 lb 19 oz the same as 4 lb 9 oz?
Job 6 · Check the Smudge's price board on the scale
Your job

The Smudge chalked a note on Mrs. Mabel's price board: 3 lb 19 oz = 4 lb 9 oz. If customers believe it, every bag in the store will be weighed wrong. Is 3 lb 19 oz really the same as 4 lb 9 oz?

What to do
  1. Put 3 lb 19 oz on the scale: 3 one-pound weights and 19 one-ounce weights.
  2. Read where the needle stops: first the pounds, then the ounce marks after them.
  3. Type the reading in pounds and ounces. Is it 4 lb 9 oz?
The scale doesn't care how you stack the weights. It shows the real weight.
