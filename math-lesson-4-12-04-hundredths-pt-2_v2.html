Today's Mission
Every digit in a decimal has a place value. Read 75.84 as 7 tens + 5 ones + 8 tenths + 4 hundredths.
❓
Essential Question
How do you read a decimal with BOTH tenths AND hundredths?

Yesterday: hundredths from a grid. Today: decimals with multiple digits, like 75.84. Each digit has its own place value.

Reading 75.84: "seventy-five and eighty-four hundredths." The 7 is 70. The 5 is 5. The 8 is 0.8 (eight tenths). The 4 is 0.04 (four hundredths).

What you saw. Each digit's value = digit × place value. Tens place: × 10. Ones: × 1. Tenths: × 0.1. Hundredths: × 0.01. Same digit, different places, very different values.
📐 Place Value in Decimals

Tens | Ones · Tenths | Hundredths. Each place is 10 times smaller than the one to its left.

Expanded form: 75.84 = 70 + 5 + 0.8 + 0.04. Each digit times its place value, summed.

Read aloud with "and" for the decimal point: 75.84 = "seventy-five AND eighty-four hundredths."
