One whole is 10 tenths. 1.2 is 10 tenths + 2 tenths = 12 tenths. And 2.3 can be 2 ones and 3 tenths, or 1 one and 13 tenths. Same amount, different pieces. You’ll use that last idea today.

📐 What You Just Saw

Start with the tenths. If the top number doesn’t have enough tenths to take away, break 1 one into 10 tenths. That’s regrouping.

In the column, cross out the ones digit and write one less above it. Cross out the tenths digit and write the new, bigger number of tenths above it. Then subtract the tenths, then the ones.

Never flip the digits around. In 1.2 − 0.5, the 5 tenths come out of the 2 tenths, not the other way.

Quick check: When you subtract, the answer is always smaller than the number you started with. Jack’s 1.3 L is more than the 1.2 L in the pitcher, so it can’t be right.

Step 1. Tenths: 2 tenths, need to take 6. Not enough.
Step 2. Break 1 one into 10 tenths. Now 3 ones and 12 tenths.
Step 3. Tenths: 12 − 6 = 6. Ones: 3 − 2 = 1. There is 1.6 L left.
Check by adding. 1.6 + 2.6 = 4.2. That’s what Mia started with. ✓
