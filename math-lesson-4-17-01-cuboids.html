📐 What You Just Saw

Every cuboid has 6 faces, 12 edges and 8 vertices, no matter how long, wide or tall it is. Its faces are rectangles that come in 3 matching pairs.

A cube is a special cuboid where every edge is the same length, so every face is a square.

Watch out: Don’t mix up the parts. Faces are flat (6), edges are lines (12), and vertices are points (8). Theo counted faces and called them edges.

Worked Example: A Smart Way to Count Edges
No double counting
Problem

Cora needs to tape every edge of a toy-drive box. How many edges will she tape?

Top: the top face has 4 edges.
Bottom: the bottom face has 4 edges.
Standing up: 4 edges join the top corners to the bottom corners.
Total: 4 + 4 + 4 = 12 edges.
A
Atlas
"Same trick for corners: 4 on top and 4 on the bottom. That’s 8 vertices."
C
Cora
"If I counted 4 edges on every face, I’d get 24. That’s double, because each edge touches two faces."
