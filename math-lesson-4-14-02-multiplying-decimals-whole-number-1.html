📐 What You Just Saw

To multiply a number like 1.4 by a whole number, start with the tenths. 3 × 4 tenths = 12 tenths. Only one digit fits in a place, so trade 10 tenths for 1 one. Write 2 in the tenths place and a little 1 above the ones.

Then multiply the ones and add the little number: 3 × 1 = 3, and 3 + 1 = 4. The decimal point comes straight down. 3 × 1.4 = 4.2.

Watch out: 3 × 1.4 is not 3.12. That answer squeezes 12 tenths into the tenths place, but only one digit fits there. And 3.12 is less than 3.4, the weight of just 2 bags and a bit. 12 tenths is 1 one and 2 tenths, so the answer is 4.2.

Worked Example: Six Pitchers of Punch
More drinks for the sale
Problem

One pitcher holds 1.3 L of punch. Mia fills 6 pitchers. How many liters of punch is that?

1
1.3

Estimate. 1.3 is between 1 and 2. 6 × 1 = 6 and 6 × 2 = 12, so the answer is between 6 and 12.
Tenths. 6 × 3 tenths = 18 tenths. Trade 10 tenths for 1 one. Write 8, put a little 1 above the ones.
Ones. 6 × 1 one = 6 ones. Add the little 1: 7 ones.
Answer. 6 × 1.3 = 7.8 L. That’s between 6 and 12. ✓
