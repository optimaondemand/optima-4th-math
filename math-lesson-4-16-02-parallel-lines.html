📐 What You Just Saw

Parallel lines go in the same direction and stay the same distance apart everywhere. They never meet, no matter how far they go.

If the gap is even a little different at the two ends, the lines will meet somewhere. Mark parallel lines with matching arrow marks.

🗂️
Three Ways Lines Can Go
Perpendicular, parallel, or neither
Lines that…NameReal life
meet at square cornersperpendiculara plus sign, the corner of a window
never meet, same gap everywhereparalleltrain tracks, notebook lines
meet, but not at square cornersintersecting, but not perpendicularthe letter X, scissors
Watch out: Lines that haven’t crossed yet aren’t automatically parallel. Measure the gap at both ends. If the gaps are different, the lines will meet farther along.

Worked Example: Parallel Sides in Town Lots
Counting pairs of parallel sides
Problem

The town has three building lots: a rectangle, a trapezoid and a triangle. How many pairs of parallel sides does each lot have?

Rectangle: top and bottom are parallel (one arrow each). Left and right are parallel (two arrows each). 2 pairs.
Trapezoid: only the top and bottom are parallel. The slanted sides would meet if you extended them. 1 pair.
Triangle: every side leans toward the others. They all meet. 0 pairs.

Hazel
