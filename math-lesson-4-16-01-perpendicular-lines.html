📐 What You Just Saw

Perpendicular lines cross at a right angle. When they do, all four corners around the crossing are right angles.

Perpendicular lines don’t have to go straight up and straight across. Lines on a slant can be perpendicular too. Check with a square corner.

🗂️
Lines That Meet, Lines That Don’t
Sorting what you see on the map
What the lines doNameOn the map
Cross at square cornersPerpendicularElm St and Main St
Cross, but not at square cornersIntersecting (not perpendicular)Oak St and Main St
Never cross(Next lesson: parallel)Two streets side by side
Watch out: Every pair of perpendicular lines intersects, but not every pair of intersecting lines is perpendicular. Oak Street crosses Main Street, but its corners aren’t square.

Worked Example: Letters on the Town Sign
Perpendicular segments in letters
Problem

The town sign uses the letters L, T, V and X. Which letters have perpendicular segments?

L: the two segments meet at a square corner. Perpendicular ✓
T: the top bar and the post meet at square corners. Perpendicular ✓
V: the segments meet, but the corner is much narrower than a square corner. Not perpendicular.
X: the segments cross on a slant. Two corners are skinny and two are wide. Not perpendicular.
Answer: L and T.
