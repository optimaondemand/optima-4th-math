Things you can see around you.

How many square corners does a sheet of paper have?
You face the board and turn around to face the back wall. How many quarter turns is that?
How many quarter turns bring you all the way around?
✅ 4, 2 and 4.
A quarter turn makes a square corner. Two quarter turns is a half turn. Four quarter turns bring you all the way around. Today you’ll see that angles are turns too.

Back to Theo’s question from the warm-up

Vertex on vertex, bottom ray on bottom ray. Which angle opens wider?

✅ Q is the bigger angle.
Q’s arms are short, but its opening sticks out past P’s top ray. The size of an angle is how far it turns. Long arms don’t make a bigger turn. Theo’s idea was a good guess, but the arms fooled him.
🔄 Look at the openings.
📐 What You Just Saw

An angle is a turn from one ray to the other, around the vertex. The size of the angle is how far it turns. The length of the arms doesn’t change it.

Compare any angle to a square corner. Less is acute. The same is right. More, but not a straight line, is obtuse. A straight line is a straight angle.

🔁

Back to Theo’s ramps: Angle Q is bigger. It opens about twice as wide as angle P. Theo looked at the arms, but long arms don’t make an angle bigger. Only the turn between the arms does.

Four Kinds of Angles
Each one on a clock face
NameCompared to a square cornerOn a clock
AcuteSmaller1:00 or 2:00
RightExactly the same (a quarter turn)3:00 or 9:00
ObtuseBigger, but not a straight line4:00 or 5:00
StraightTwo square corners in a line (a half turn)6:00
Watch out: Don’t judge an angle by its arms. A tiny angle drawn with long arms is still tiny. Look only at the opening near the vertex.

Worked Example: The Clock at 4:00
Hands are rays, the center is the vertex
Problem

The skate park opens at 4:00. What kind of angle do the clock hands make at 4:00?

Find the rays. The minute hand points to 12. The hour hand points to 4. The center of the clock is the vertex.
Compare to a square corner. At 3:00 the hands make a right angle. 4 is past 3, so the opening is wider.
Is it a straight line? No. That would be 6:00.
Name it. Bigger than a right angle, not a straight line: obtuse.
