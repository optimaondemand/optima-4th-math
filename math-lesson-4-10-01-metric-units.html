The Math Crew: Two Ways
How long is Amy's counter: 3 × 1 m 65 cm?

The Crew found the length of Amy's counter two different ways. Read both. Which way would you pick?

A
Atlas: split the units
"I split 1 m 65 cm into 1 m and 65 cm. 3 × 1 m = 3 m. 3 × 65 cm = 195 cm. That's 3 m 195 cm, but 195 cm is more than 1 m. 195 cm = 1 m 95 cm, so the counter is 4 m 95 cm."
F
Felix: one unit first
"I change it all to centimeters. 1 m 65 cm = 165 cm. 3 × 165 cm = 495 cm. Then I change back: 495 cm = 4 m 95 cm. Same answer as Atlas!"
H
Hazel: draw it
"I draw a bar with 3 equal parts, one for each plank. Each part is 1 m 65 cm. The whole bar is the counter."
C
Cora: trade when it gets too big
"When the small unit reaches 100 cm, trade it for 1 m. For grams and milliliters, trade 1,000 for 1 kg or 1 L. That trade is how you regroup."
💡

Crew rule: Draw the story (Hazel). Pick a way: split or one unit (Atlas, Felix). Regroup and check the units (Cora).

📐 The Market Measures Board

Length: 10 mm = 1 cm. 100 cm = 1 m. 1,000 m = 1 km.

Mass: 1,000 g = 1 kg. (Millie's flour)

Capacity: 1,000 mL = 1 L. (Amy's juice jugs)

Two units at once? Pick a way. Split: work on each unit, then regroup. One unit: change it all to the small unit, work, then change back.

Exact words: regroup means trading small units for a big one (100 cm for 1 m) or a big one for small ones (1 kg for 1,000 g).

Worked Example: Mo's Board
Subtract two-unit amounts, two ways
Problem

Mo has a board 3 m 20 cm long. He cuts off 1 m 45 cm. How long is the piece that is left?

20 cm − 45 cm won't work. Regroup first: trade 1 m for 100 cm.
Split the units (Atlas)
  1. Regroup: 3 m 20 cm = 2 m 120 cm.
  2. Centimeters: 120 cm − 45 cm = 75 cm.
  3. Meters: 2 m − 1 m = 1 m.
  4. Answer: 1 m 75 cm.
One unit first (Felix)
  1. Change: 3 m 20 cm = 320 cm. 1 m 45 cm = 145 cm.
  2. Subtract: 320 cm − 145 cm = 175 cm.
  3. Change back: 175 cm = 1 m 75 cm.

Check (Cora): 1 m 75 cm + 1 m 45 cm = 2 m 120 cm = 3 m 20 cm ✓
