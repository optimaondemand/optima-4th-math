Today's Mission
Find the two neighbors on the number line. Pick the one your decimal is closer to. If exactly halfway, round UP.
❓
Essential Question
How do you round a decimal to the nearest tenth or whole?

Pick the rounding place (tenth, whole, or hundredth). Find the two neighbors. Which one is your decimal closer to?

Halfway rule: if the decimal sits exactly between, always round UP. 1.55 rounded to the nearest tenth = 1.6.

Warm-Up: Closer to Which?
Each decimal sits between two neighbors. Pick the closer one.
~5 min

Three decimals to round.

6.26 to nearest tenth (6.2 or 6.3?)
12.7 to nearest whole (12 or 13?)
1.55 to nearest tenth (halfway!)
Rounded: 6.26 → 6.3 (past halfway 6.25). 12.7 → 13 (past halfway 12.5). 1.55 → 1.6 (exactly halfway → round UP).
✏️ How is 1.55 different from 6.26? Both round up — but for different reasons.
🤔
Think About It
Two rounds, one number.
📐

Round 3.42 two different ways:

(a) To the nearest tenth — neighbors are 3.4 and 3.5.
(b) To the nearest whole — neighbors are 3 and 4.

🧠

(a) Halfway between 3.4 and 3.5 is 3.45. 3.42 is BEFORE halfway → round DOWN → 3.4.
(b) Halfway between 3 and 4 is 3.5. 3.42 is BEFORE halfway → round DOWN → 3.

✏️ Why does the rounding answer change depending on the PLACE?

📐 Rounding Steps

1. Pick the place. Nearest tenth, nearest whole, nearest hundredth.

2. Find the two neighbors. The "nice" numbers your decimal sits between at that place.

3. Find halfway. Exactly between the neighbors.

4. Pick the closer neighbor. Halfway always rounds UP.
