📐 What You Just Saw

A cuboid’s 12 edges come in 3 groups of 4 parallel edges: 4 lengths, 4 widths and 4 heights. Edges in the same group are the same length.

Total edge length = 4 × length + 4 × width + 4 × height. A cube has 12 equal edges, so its total is 12 × one edge.

Opposite faces are parallel and the same size. Faces that share an edge are perpendicular.

Worked Example: A Wire Cube
Working backward
Problem

Cora bends 96 cm of wire into a cube frame with nothing left over. How long is each edge?

Step 1: A cube has 12 edges, and they are all the same length.
Step 2: The 96 cm is shared equally by 12 edges: 96 ÷ 12 = 8.
Answer: Each edge is 8 cm.
Check: 12 × 8 = 96. ✓
