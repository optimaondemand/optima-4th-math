🥽 In VR
Step 2 of 7 · Go in
🔗 First time only: connect Canvas at xr.optimaed.com. The steps are in Having trouble in VR? below.

Open the On-Demand app. Click Explore. Type Castle in Filter environments. Click Medieval Castle (Day), then Begin Solo.

Step 3 of 7 · Build the floor

Walk to an open, flat spot. Open your menu (the top button on your right controller) and click Models.

Part A: lay down 1 Flat.

Part B: line up 4 Rods side by side. Push them against the back edge of the Flat, on its left side.

✋ Grab a model with the palm trigger. Keep every piece its real size.
Step 4 of 7 · Write the area with models

How many small squares cover the whole floor? Find the area of each part. Then add.

In front of the floor, make a line of models: Part A's area, a Plus Sign, Part B's area, an Equal Sign, then the total.

🗣 Say it out loud: how did you find the area of each part?
VR onlyStep 5 of 7 · Extra, if you have time

Slide the 4 Rods to a new spot along the Flat. They must still touch it. Does the floor need the same number of tiles? Why?

Step 6 of 7 · Snap or record, then submit

Stand back so the floor and your answer both fit. Open your menu, click Library, then Camera.

Take a photo, or switch to Video and record up to 30 seconds.

Click the Canvas tab and pick 4.11.03 VR · The Castle Floor. Click Choose from My Files, pick your photo or video, click Use this file, then Submit to Canvas. Look for the green Submitted tag.

One photo or one video is the only thing you turn in.

🔁 Want to fix it? Move the models and submit a new one.
✅ After
Step 7 of 7 · Check and leave

Press the Meta button, then click Quit. Open the Canvas assignment. Not there? Open Having trouble in VR? below.

⭐ Full points: Your photo or video (30 seconds or less) shows the floor with 1 Flat and 4 Rods touching, and your answer in Number models: Part A's area + Part B's area = the total.

Sketchbook Day · no headset

Tile the floor sketch

Leonardo · Master of the WorkshopNo headset today? Tile the floor in my sketchbook. Then write me a paragraph about how you solved it.
👀 What am I looking at? This is Leonardo's sketch of the floor. Each small square on the grid is 1 square unit. A Flat covers 100 small squares. A Rod covers 10.
What to do: Drag or tap the pieces onto the sketch, one part at a time. Then build your equation with the number tiles.
✍️ Your math paragraph for Leonardo
A math paragraph tells how you solved it. Write 4 sentences or more (45 words or more):
  1. What did you build, and what is the answer?
  2. How did you solve it? Use the numbers.
  3. How do you know it is right?
  4. Use 2 of our math words.
