📐 What You Just Saw

To multiply tenths or hundredths by a whole number, multiply the digits like a times-table fact, then keep the same kind of piece. 3 × 2 tenths = 6 tenths, so 3 × 0.2 = 0.6.

If you get 10 or more of a piece, trade: 12 tenths = 1.2, and 12 hundredths = 0.12.

🗂️
Same Fact, Different Places
Atlas’s pattern. Read across each row.
Whole numbersTenthsHundredths
3 × 2 = 63 × 0.2 = 0.63 × 0.02 = 0.06
4 × 3 = 124 × 0.3 = 1.24 × 0.03 = 0.12
5 × 2 = 105 × 0.2 = 1.0, which is 15 × 0.02 = 0.10, which is 0.1
Watch out: 4 × 0.3 is not 0.12. Four groups of 3 tenths is 12 tenths, and 12 tenths is 1.2. 0.12 would be 12 hundredths, a much smaller amount.

Worked Example: Six Batches of Cookies
More baking for the sale
Problem

One batch of cookies uses 0.3 kg of flour. Mia makes 6 batches. How much flour does she use?

Say it. 6 groups of 3 tenths.
Multiply. 6 × 3 = 18, so 6 groups of 3 tenths = 18 tenths.
Trade. 18 tenths = 10 tenths + 8 tenths = 1 one and 8 tenths.
Write it. 6 × 0.3 = 1.8 kg.
Check. 0.3 is about a third of a kilogram. 6 thirds is 2. 1.8 is close to 2. ✓
