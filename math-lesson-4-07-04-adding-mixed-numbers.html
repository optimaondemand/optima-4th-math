Essential Question
Which is bigger: 512 or 712? What about 23 versus 712? How can you tell?

512 vs 712 — same bottom, so bigger top wins → 7/12. 23 vs 712 — different bottoms, so rename them with a common bottom (23 = 812), then compare. 812 > 712, so 23 > 7/12.

Any division problem can be written as a fraction. a ÷ b = a/b. Try it with 3 ÷ 4: written as a fraction, that's 3/4. Three things shared four ways = each piece is 34 of one.

📐 Mixed + Mixed Procedure

Step 1 — Check fractions. If denominators differ, rename to a common denominator.

Step 2 — Add the WHOLES column. Just whole + whole.

Step 3 — Add the FRACTIONS column. Same-denom rule (Lesson 7.01).

Step 4 — Regroup if needed. If the fraction sum is improper, convert and carry the extra 1 to the wholes column.

Step 5 — Simplify the fraction part.

Worked Example: 3 34 + 3 712
Unlike denominators plus a regroup.
Problem

Find 3 34 + 3 712. Express in simplest form.

Watch the Math Crew solve it together:

Step 1 — Rename. 34 × 33 = 9/12. So 3 34 = 3 912.
Step 2 — Wholes column. 3 + 3 = 6.
Step 3 — Fractions column. 912 + 712 = 1612 = 1 412.
Step 4 — Carry. 6 + 1 = 7. Fraction left: 412 = 13.
Final: 7 13.
⚖️ Three Outcomes
NO REGROUP
Like denom, proper sum.
3 27 + 2 47 = 5 67
REGROUP
Like denom, improper sum. Carry the 1.
4 58 + 7 78 = 12 12
RENAME + REGROUP
Unlike denom + improper. Both.
3 34 + 3 712 = 7 13

Improper or mixed — same amount, two ways. Now practice reading both.
