Readiness Check: Two Pieces
Whole units, then a fraction of a unit.
~3 min

1 foot = 12 inches. Type a whole number.

2 feet =inches
34 foot =inches
✅ 24 inches and 9 inches.
2 × 12 = 24. 34 × 12 = 9, because 12 ÷ 4 = 3 and 3 groups of 3 make 9. Hold on to those two pieces. Together they make 2 34 feet.
🔄 One piece at a time.
Whole feet: each foot is 12 inches, so multiply the number of feet by 12. A fraction of a foot: split 12 inches into equal groups (the denominator), then take some of them (the numerator). For 34: 4 groups of 3 inches, take 3 groups.

Mixed-Measure Builder · Round 1
Weigh the pumpkin
The pumpkin. Wren’s pumpkin weighs 2 34 pounds. 1 pound = 16 ounces. Build the weight, then read it in ounces.
Step 1 · Add whole pounds
Step 2 · Split one more pound into equal parts
Step 3 · What will the scale show?
ounces
✅ 44 ounces.
2 whole pounds: 2 × 16 = 32 ounces. 34 of a pound: 16 ÷ 4 = 4 ounces in each part, and 3 parts make 12 ounces. 32 + 12 = 44. How close was your Warm-Up prediction?
🔄 Two pieces, then add.
Build both pieces on the shelf first. Each full pound holds 16 ounces. For the 34 pound, split 16 ounces into 4 equal parts and take 3 of them. Change each piece to ounces, then add the two pieces.

Mixed-Measure Builder · Round 2
Same idea, different unit
The cider press. Wren’s uncle runs the cider press for 1 23 hours. 1 hour = 60 minutes. Each little block below is 5 minutes.
Step 1 · Add whole hours
Step 2 · Split one more hour into equal parts
Step 3 · How many minutes does the press run?
minutes
✅ 100 minutes.
1 whole hour is 60 minutes. 23 of an hour: 60 ÷ 3 = 20 minutes in each part, and 2 parts make 40 minutes. 60 + 40 = 100. Same two steps as the pumpkin. Only the factor changed, from 16 to 60.
🔄 Use the factor 60.
Build both pieces first. The whole hour is 60 minutes. For 23 of an hour, split 60 minutes into 3 equal parts and take 2. Then add the two pieces.

Mixed-Measure Builder · Round 3
Now go the other way
The shelf board. Wren’s dad cuts a board 42 inches long for the market table. How many feet is that? Each little block is 1 inch.
Step 1 · Mark off whole feet (12 inches each)
Step 2 · Write the board’s length as a mixed number of feet
ft
✅ 3 12 feet.
3 whole feet use 36 inches. The 6 inches left over are 6 of the 12 inches in a foot: 612 of a foot. 612 simplifies to 12. So 42 inches = 3 12 feet. Check it forward: 36 + 6 = 42. ✓
🔄 Whole feet, then the leftover.
Mark off every whole foot first. The whole number is how many full feet fit. The leftover inches go over 12, because 12 inches make a foot. Then simplify the fraction.
📐 What You Just Saw

A mixed-number measurement has two pieces: whole units and a fraction of a unit.

Big unit to small unit: change each piece with the factor, then add. 2 34 lb = 32 oz + 12 oz = 44 oz.

Small unit to big unit: count the whole units that fit. Put the leftover over the factor, then simplify. 42 in = 3 ft and 612 ft = 3 12 ft.

Worked Example: Two Pumpkins
Back to Wren’s pumpkin from the Warm-Up
Problem

At the end of the day, two pumpkins are left. One weighs 2 34 lb. The other weighs 1 lb 10 oz. How much do they weigh together? Write the answer in pounds and ounces, then as a mixed number of pounds.

Step 1. Change 2 34 lb to pounds and ounces. 34 × 16 = 12, so it is 2 lb 12 oz.
Step 2. Add like units. 2 lb 12 oz + 1 lb 10 oz = 3 lb 22 oz.
Step 3. Regroup. 22 oz = 16 oz + 6 oz = 1 lb 6 oz. So the total is 4 lb 6 oz.
Step 4. Write it as a mixed number. 6 oz = 616 lb = 38 lb. So the total is 4 38 lb.
Check. In ounces: 44 + 26 = 70. And 4 × 16 + 6 = 70. ✓
