Read the story. Decide whether to multiply, divide, add, or subtract — then do it.
❓
Essential Question
Which is bigger: 512 or 712? What about 23 versus 712? How can you tell?

512 vs 712 — same bottom, so bigger top wins → 7/12. 23 vs 712 — different bottoms, so rename them with a common bottom (23 = 812), then compare. 812 > 712, so 23 > 7/12.

Any division problem can be written as a fraction. a ÷ b = a/b. Try it with 3 ÷ 4: written as a fraction, that's 3/4. Three things shared four ways = each piece is 34 of one.

Warm-Up: Forward or Inverse?
Read the story. Decide what kind of problem it is.
~5 min

For each story, decide: are you finding a PART (forward), the OTHER PART (whole minus part), or the WHOLE (inverse)?

Story 1: A cat has 30 teeth. 25 of them are incisors. How many incisors?
Story 2: 30 of Asimah's books are nonfiction. That is 25 of her collection. How many books does she have?
Story 3: A trip is 24 miles. Carlos has driven 58 of it. How many miles are LEFT?
Story types: Story 1 = forward (whole given, find a part). Story 2 = inverse (part given, find the whole). Story 3 = other part (find whole minus 58).
✏️ What clue tells you which type?
🤔
Think About It
Same numbers, two different stories.
📐

Story A: A pool has 40 gallons. 38 of the water is salt water. How many gallons of salt water?

Story B: A pool contains 40 gallons of fresh water. 38 of all the water (fresh + salt combined) is fresh water. How many gallons TOTAL?

Which one is forward? Which one is inverse?

🧠

A is forward (whole = 40, find 38 of it = 15 gal salt). B is inverse (40 = 38 of whole, so each 18 = 40÷3... wait, that does not divide evenly! So 40 gallons fresh water as 38 of the whole means the whole is 40 ÷ 38 = 40 × 83 = 106 23 gallons. Story B leads to a fraction-answer pool.).

📐 Three Story Types

Forward: whole given, find a part. Split into denominator-many piles, take numerator-many.

Other part: whole given, find what is NOT taken. Total minus the part.

Inverse: a part given, find the whole. Divide the part by the numerator to get one pile, then multiply by the denominator.

📐
Picture It: An Inverse Bar Model
Asimah's books — work backward from the part.
Bar is split into 5 piles (denominator). 2 piles shaded = nonfiction = 30 books.
Per-pile value
30 ÷ 2 piles
= 15 books
All 5 piles
15 × 5 piles
= 75 books
Asimah has 75 books total.

Worked Example: A Two-Step Story
Mia's jug — pour, then ask what is left.
Problem

Mia's jug holds 4 liters of water. She pours 10 cups, and each cup holds 13 liter. (a) How many liters did she pour? (b) How much water is LEFT in the jug?

Watch the Math Crew solve it together:

Step 1. Poured: 10 × 13 = 10/3. Resolve: 3 13 liters.
Step 2. Left: 4 − 3 13 = 3 33 − 3 13 = 23 liter.
Sense check: Did less than 4 liters get poured? Yes (3 13 < 4). Is the leftover small? Yes (23). ✓
